Use the cheapest adequate observation. Prefer compact text/state queries for
finding controls and screenshots for visual inspection. Start scoped, then widen
when the evidence is insufficient. Keep selectors exact and request only a few
results. A truncated or unreadable search is not proof of absence. Reobserve
after a timeout; do not blindly replay an action whose result is unknown.
Use fresh refs after observations that invalidate earlier ones. Presence alone
does not prove selection. Save screenshots artifact-only when possible; load
the saved image when you need to check visual quality. Do not load broad maps
or unrelated documentation for a narrow task.
